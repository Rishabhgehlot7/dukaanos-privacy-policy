Privacy Policy for DukaanOS

Effective Date: October 11, 2026 • Version: 1.0.0

Welcome to DukaanOS ("we," "our," or "us"). We build modern, reliable, and secure operating systems for small and medium retail merchants, wholesalers, and shopkeepers across India. We are committed to protecting the privacy, accuracy, and security of your business and customer information.

This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application DukaanOS collects, uses, protects, and discloses information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information strictly required to deliver fast and reliable POS billing, digital ledger (khata), and inventory management services:

  • Merchant Account Information: Your store/business name, shopkeeper name, mobile phone number, email address, and optional GSTIN number.
  • Business & Transactional Records: Products added to inventory (names, selling prices, purchase prices, stock quantities), customer directory details (customer names, phone numbers), sales invoices, bill receipts, and credit/debit ledger entries (उधार और जमा हिसाब).
  • Authentication Information: Secure tokens provided via Google Sign-In or Firebase Authentication. We never store or access your personal passwords.

2. Device Permissions & Specific Usage

To provide high-efficiency retail functionality, DukaanOS requests the following Android permissions:

📷 Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA):
The camera is used exclusively for scanning product barcodes and QR codes to instantly add items to the POS billing cart and look up inventory. DukaanOS does NOT record videos, snap photos, capture merchant faces, or store any camera imagery.

  • Internet & Network State (android.permission.INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Required to verify connectivity and sync encrypted business records with our cloud database (Google Firebase) in real-time.
  • Bluetooth / Nearby Devices: If enabled, used solely to connect to standard Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal receipt printers for printing paper bills.

3. Data Storage & Security Practices

Your business continuity and confidentiality are our highest priorities:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the DukaanOS mobile app and our servers are encrypted using modern Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS).
  • Encryption at Rest: Cloud data is stored on Google Cloud / Firebase Firestore servers with industry-standard AES-256 encryption.
  • Zero Commercial Sharing: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade your shop records, sales data, customer phone numbers, or khata balances with third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. Third-Party Services

DukaanOS integrates with industry-standard Google infrastructure to provide reliable services:

  • Google Firebase Authentication: For secure, one-click sign-in without passwords.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: For automated real-time database synchronization and backups.
  • WhatsApp Integration: The app utilizes standard deep-links to allow you to send bill receipts and ledger summaries directly via your own installed WhatsApp application. DukaanOS does not access your personal WhatsApp chats.

5. Data Retention & User Rights (Account & Data Deletion)

You maintain full ownership and control over your store's information:

  • You can edit or delete individual transactions, bills, customers, and inventory items directly within the app at any time.
  • Account & Data Deletion: If you wish to permanently delete your account and erase all associated business data from our servers, you may send a written request to boostengine001@gmail.com or rishabh3x@gmail.com. All associated records will be purged within 30 days.

6. Children's Privacy

DukaanOS is designed solely for business management and retail commercial operations. Our app is not directed toward children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect app enhancements or statutory updates. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated effective date.
